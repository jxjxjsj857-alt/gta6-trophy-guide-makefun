Roadmap to the platinum — about 200 hours

PowerPyx rates the full set 4 out of 10 in difficulty and about 200 hours, with one playthrough enough and no online trophies. There are no difficulty settings, so no trophy asks for a harder mode. Work the stages in order; most of stage 4 finishes itself while you do the rest.

    Stages are built from the official descriptions: chapter and mission trophies first, side-character finales next, then the collectible and activity counts, and the 100% checklist last.

    ⚠ Missable trophies: one, and only one

    Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot across Leonida (18 spots, 10 locations) — is the only trophy reported as potentially missable. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning; she says she is going to meditate, follows her to the balcony and choose Accept. That first spot is reported as gone after Chapter 1. Every other trophy can be earned in free roam after the story, and nothing on the 100% checklist is missable. Source: PowerPyx trophy guide & roadmap; GTABase agrees.

    Tip: keep a separate save slot before the final mission — there is free roam after the story, but no mission replay.
